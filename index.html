Three ways to log a meal

Whichever is quickest right now. The typing is done for you.

Snap it

Point the camera at your plate. Each food is named on its own, with portions you can correct.

Say it

“Two eggs, toast and a coffee.” Rough amounts are fine, and several meals at once too.

Scan it

A barcode gives you the label’s exact numbers, straight from the packet.

Every meal comes back with calories, macros and 19 vitamins & minerals, measured against reference amounts worked out for you. Your meals stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud; there is no account and no ads.
