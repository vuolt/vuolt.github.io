Getting started

Open a PDF

  1. On the home screen, tap Open a PDF.
  2. Pick the file — from iCloud Drive, Downloads, or anywhere the Files app can reach.
  3. It opens to read. Scroll through it, pinch to zoom, and use the buttons along the bottom to fill, mark up or organise it.

You can also send a PDF to the app from another one: in Mail or Files, tap Share and choose PDF Offline Studio. On a Mac, drag a PDF onto the app's window.

Save or share your work

  1. Tap Save at the top right.
  2. Choose Save to Files to keep a copy, Share to send it (Mail, Messages, AirDrop…), or Save with a password to lock the copy.

The app doesn't keep documents. Files you open are copied into a temporary workspace that is wiped every time the app starts — so save anything you want to keep.

Print

  1. With the document open, tap the Print button at the top. On a phone it's inside ⋯ (More tools).
  2. Choose your printer in the print panel.

If you've drawn, written or signed on the page, tap Apply first — the app asks you to, so marks aren't left off the printout.

“You have a file that was never saved”

If the app closed while a document still had changes, it offers that document the next time it starts. Choose Save to Files or Share to keep it, or Discard to delete it. Because the app keeps nothing, anything not saved there is gone.

Pages

Merge PDFs into one

  1. On the home screen, tap Merge and pick two or more PDFs.
  2. Drag the files into the order you want.
  3. Tap Merge n files, then Save.

If one of the files has a password, you'll be asked for it once. Cancel, and nothing is merged.

Split a PDF into several files

  1. Tap Split and pick the PDF. It opens cut at every page.
  2. Use Every page, Every 2, Every 5 … or tap Clear and then the gaps between pages to cut exactly where you want.
  3. Tap Split into n files.
  4. Save one part with Save this one, all of them with Save all as .zip, or send them with Share all.

Keep only some pages

  1. Tap Extract and pick the PDF.
  2. Tap the pages you want to keep.
  3. Tap Keep n pages, then Save.

Delete pages

  1. Tap Delete and pick the PDF.
  2. Tap the pages to remove, then Delete n pages.
  3. Save. Until you leave the document, Undo brings the pages back.

Rotate pages

  1. Tap Rotate and pick the PDF.
  2. Tap the pages that are the wrong way round, then Rotate n pages and choose 90°, 180° or 270°.
  3. Save.

Put pages in a new order

  1. Tap Reorder and pick the PDF.
  2. Press and hold a page, then drag it to its new place.
  3. Tap Apply new order, then Save.

See every page at once

With a document open, tap All pages. From the grid you can tap a page to jump to it, or switch between Extract, Delete, Rotate and Reorder without starting again.

Mark up & forms

Fill in a form

  1. Open the PDF. If it has form fields, the first button along the bottom is called Fill — make sure it's selected.
  2. Tap a field and type, or tap a tick box or a choice.
  3. Tap Next field to move on, or Done when you're finished.
  4. Save. The values are saved into the form's own fields, so other apps can read and change them.

No Fill button? Then the PDF has no real form fields — it's a picture of a form. Use Text to type onto it instead.

Sign a document

  1. Open the PDF and tap Sign along the bottom.
  2. Sign with your finger, Apple Pencil or mouse, then tap Use signature.
  3. Tap the page where it should go. To move or resize it afterwards, use Select.
  4. Tap Apply, then Save.

Turn on Remember this signature to reuse it. It's kept in your device's encrypted keychain — never as a file, and never uploaded.

Draw on a page

  1. Tap Draw along the bottom.
  2. Draw with one finger. Use two fingers to scroll or zoom while the pen is out.
  3. Tap Apply to add your marks to the document, then Save.

Highlight text

  1. Tap Highlight and drag across what you want to highlight.
  2. Tap Apply, then Save.

Type text onto a page

  1. Tap Text, then tap the page where the text should go.
  2. Type, and press Return or Add to keep it.
  3. Tap Apply, then Save.

Move, resize or delete a mark

  1. Tap Select and drag a box around the mark.
  2. Drag it to move it, pinch to resize it, or tap Delete.

After Apply, one Undo takes back everything that Apply added.

Add a watermark

  1. Tap Watermark and pick the PDF.
  2. Type the text, and choose Once, centred or Tiled (repeated across the page, harder to crop out) and how strong it should be.
  3. Tap Apply to every page, then Save.

Stamp a document

  1. Tap Stamp and pick the PDF.
  2. Choose a stamp — Draft, Confidential, Approved and more — or Make your own with your own words and colours — and where it goes.
  3. Tap Apply to every page, then Save.

A stamp you make is kept on your device for next time.

Number the pages

  1. Tap Page numbers and pick the PDF.
  2. Choose where they go and the number to start at. Turn on Skip the first page for documents with a cover.
  3. Tap Apply to every page, then Save.

Make marks permanent (Flatten)

A signature or note the app adds is a separate object in the file, so whoever you send it to could move or delete it. Flatten PDF redraws every page as a picture, so your marks become part of the page.

  1. Tap Flatten PDF (or choose it from ⋯ with the document open).
  2. Choose how sharp, tap Flatten PDF, then Save.

The cost: text stops being selectable and searchable, and the file gets bigger. Your original is untouched.

Security

Remove sensitive information (Redact)

  1. Tap Redact and pick the PDF (or choose it from ⋯ with the document open).
  2. Drag a box over everything that should go. Choose Bar (black) or Erase (white).
  3. Tap Redact n areas and confirm with Remove.
  4. Save.

Redact removes what's under the boxes — it isn't just covered up. Don't use Draw for this: a drawn black box sits on top, and anyone can still select and copy the text underneath.

Add a password

  1. Tap Lock and pick the PDF — or open any document.
  2. Tap Save, then Save with a password.
  3. Type the password and tap Protect.

Keep the password somewhere safe: there is no way to recover it from the file, and the app never stores it.

Remove a password you know

  1. Tap Unlock and pick the PDF.
  2. Type its password. The copy you're working on no longer has one.
  3. Save to keep that unlocked copy.

Why does it ask for the password every time?

On purpose. A password you type is used to open the file and forgotten straight afterwards — it is never written to disk and there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Convert

Make a PDF smaller (Compress)

  1. Tap Compress and pick the PDF.
  2. Choose Smallest, Balanced or Best quality.
  3. Tap Compress, then Save.

Compress rebuilds each page as a picture, which is where the saving comes from — so text in a compressed copy can't be selected or searched. Scans and photos shrink the most; if a file is already small, the app says so and changes nothing.

Turn photos or scans into a PDF

  1. Tap Images to PDF, choose Photos or Files, and pick your images.
  2. Drag them into order. Add more images adds more.
  3. Choose Fit to image (each page the shape of its picture) or Paper size (every page A4 or Letter, best for printing).
  4. Tap Make a PDF of n pages, then Save.

JPEG photos go in exactly as they are, with no loss of quality, and portrait photos come out the right way up.

Turn a PDF into images

  1. Tap PDF to images and pick the PDF.
  2. Choose JPEG (smaller, for scans and photos) or PNG (lossless, for text and diagrams), and a size.
  3. Tap Convert to images, then Share them or Save zip.

Word, Excel or PowerPoint?

The app can't turn a PDF into an editable Word, Excel or PowerPoint file. Doing that well takes a converter far too large for a phone, so every app that offers it sends your document to a server — and this app is built without any permission to reach the internet. If you need one, use a converter you already trust, and read what it says about your files first.

Getting around

Find a word

  1. With a document open, tap Find (or press ⌘ F).
  2. Type the word. Use the arrows to go to the next or previous match.

Find only works on real text. A scanned page, or one that was compressed, flattened or redacted, is a picture and can't be searched.

Jump to a page

Tap the page number (for example 3 / 12), slide to the page you want and tap Go. Or tap All pages and tap the page.

Keep your favourite tools at the top

On the home screen, press and hold a tool to add it to Favourites. Press and hold it again to remove it.

Change the language

Tap the globe icon at the top of the home screen and pick a language: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano or Português — or Same as your device.

Keyboard shortcuts (Mac, and iPad with a keyboard)

  • ⌘ F Find · Esc close Find or cancel
  • ⌘ S Save · ⌘ P Print · ⌘ Z Undo
  • ⌘ + / ⌘ − Zoom in and out

Troubleshooting

I can't type into a form

Make sure Fill is selected along the bottom — while Draw, Text or another tool is chosen, taps go to that tool instead. If there's no Fill button at all, the PDF has no real form fields; use Text to type onto it.

My signature or drawing is hidden behind a form field

Some viewers draw form fields on top of everything else on the page, so a mark that overlaps a field can end up underneath it. Place signatures and drawings beside fields rather than over them — or, once the form is filled in, use Flatten PDF so everything becomes part of the page.

The person I sent it to can move my signature

Until a document is flattened, marks are separate objects that other apps can select. Use Flatten PDF before sending.

I drew a black box, but the text is still there

Drawing covers text; it doesn't remove it. Use Redact, which removes what's under the box.

A file won't open

If the app says the file doesn't look like a readable PDF, it's usually a download that didn't finish, or a file named .pdf that is really something else. Try downloading it again. Locked files ask for their password — see why every time.

Compress didn't make it smaller

Mostly-text PDFs are already compact, and the app won't hand back a file that came out bigger. Scans and photos shrink the most. Try Smallest.

I can't select or search text any more

Compress, Flatten and Redact rebuild pages as pictures, which is how they work. Keep the original if you'll need the text.

Where did my file go?

The app keeps nothing between sessions: its workspace is wiped every time it starts. Your original file is never changed — the edited copy is wherever you saved it with Save to Files.

Does the app use the internet?

No. It's built without permission to reach the network at all, so it can't send a document anywhere, even by mistake. Links like Rate or More apps open your browser; the app itself still makes no connections.
