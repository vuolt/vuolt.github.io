Not medical or nutritional advice

This is the important one. Meal Metrics is a calorie and nutrient counter, not a medical device, and nothing in it is advice about you.

  • The reference amounts shown are population reference values calculated from your height, weight, age and sex using standard public formulas. They are not targets, not a prescription, and not personalised to your health.
  • Nutrient figures come from the USDA FoodData Central database and, for packaged products, from Open Food Facts. Where a food is missing from both, the app shows an estimate and labels it as one.
  • Portion weights are estimates made by a model looking at a photograph or reading a sentence. They can be wrong, sometimes substantially. Every number in the app can be corrected by you, and where accuracy matters you should weigh your food.
  • Do not use this app to make decisions about medication, a medical condition, or a clinical diet. Speak to a doctor or a registered dietitian.
